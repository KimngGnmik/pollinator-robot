Hive: the official BIOBUZZ HIVE (manual §9.6): pivot 43.95 in up, arm tilted 30°, upward cell opening 53.5–65.6 in above the tiles, facing the robot. Hive distance = front bumper to the hive pivot. Auto speed picks the exit speed that puts the ball through the middle of the opening.
Orange = adjustable hood exit lip. The CAD hood is fixed and launches at 60°; the lip shows how far the hood has to wrap for other angles (the ball leaves where the hood ends: exit point = 180° − launch angle round the wrap). Dashed line = where the next shot will go. Timing is slowed down so you can follow the ball. Balls are treated as stiff, so the soft flywheel tread takes the squeeze (the ball overlapping the wheel = tread compression). Flywheel dip per shot in rapid fire is an estimate.
